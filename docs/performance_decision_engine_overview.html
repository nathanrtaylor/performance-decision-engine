How it works — in four steps

Gather the facts

It pulls the performance data you already have into a clean, dated snapshot — so every decision is tied to exactly what was known at the time.

Weigh what matters

It compares each person to the agreed benchmarks and to their peers, focusing only on genuine gaps — never on strengths — and weights them by the priorities the business has set.

Decide — or hold back

It picks the single most worthwhile action. When nothing is worth flagging, it says so on purpose, with a reason — never a random filler suggestion.

Explain it

Every recommendation comes with a plain-language receipt: why this, why now, and why not the alternatives — ready to share with the person delivering it.

Why you can trust it

🎯

You set the priorities

What matters most is a business setting, not something buried in code. Priorities can change often without weakening the rigor.

🔁

Same answer every time

The same data and settings always produce the same decision. No black-box drift, no surprises.

🧾

Every decision is explained

Each recommendation carries its reasoning, so it can be reviewed, questioned, and trusted — not just followed.

🛡️

People stay in control

Changes to benchmarks and patterns are proposed for review and only take effect with a named person's sign-off. It never quietly rewrites its own rules.

Human judgment stays in charge. The engine is built to make good decisions fast and repeatable — not to remove people from the loop. When it suggests changing those benchmarks or spotting a new pattern, a person reviews and approves before anything changes.
