Our Training Decision Engine

We show every new hire
exactly what to retake.

Our onboarding produces a mountain of practice and coursework data. We built this to turn it into one clear plan per trainee — what to go back and redo, and whether they're on pace to graduate — with the reasoning attached.

Per-trainee On-pace tracking Explainable Trainer-ready

In onboarding we've never had a data problem — we've had a "what do we do about it" problem. Our engine reads each trainee's simulations and coursework, decides the one thing most worth acting on, and explains why — the same way for every class.

What it gives us

🎯

The exact retake

Not "you're behind" — the specific learning block and skills to redo, with the end-of-block test call to clear before moving on. And when a trainee is doing fine, we say so, so no one wastes a redo they don't need.

📈

On pace, or not

We track each trainee's actual block completion against the expected schedule, so we can see — per person and per class — who's on track, who's ahead, and who needs a nudge, in time to act.

🤝

A clean graduation hand-off

When someone clears every block, we generate a ready-for-coach summary: what they mastered and the couple of skills to keep an eye on in their first live calls.

🧩

The full picture

Skills roll up under the learning block that teaches them, alongside any recent coaching the trainee has had — so a trainer sees the whole story in one place, not five reports.

How it works — in four steps

We gather the facts

We pull each class's simulation and coursework results into a clean, dated snapshot, tied to the roster — who's in the class, their trainer, and when they started.

We measure against our bar

We compare each trainee to the skill bar and the block schedule we set for the program — focusing only on genuine gaps, and only on blocks they've actually reached.

We decide the retake — or confirm on-track

We surface the specific block and skills to redo, or confirm the trainee is on pace and needs nothing right now. Never a vague flag, never a filler task.

We explain and hand it off

Every call comes with its reasoning in plain language, in a per-trainee view a trainer can copy, email, or print to share with a coach.

Why we trust it

🎚️

We set the bar

The skill thresholds, the block schedule, and how far a retake reaches back are our settings — adjustable as the program evolves, without rebuilding anything.

🔁

Same answer every time

The same data and settings always produce the same plan. Every class is judged consistently — no drift, no surprises.

🧾

Every call is explained

Each retake carries its "why," so trainers and trainees can see the reasoning — and we can stand behind it.

🛡️

We stay in control

It recommends; people decide. It won't send anyone back over material they've already remediated, and it never quietly changes our bar.

What it means for you

Executives

Our judgment stays in charge. We built this to make good onboarding decisions fast and repeatable — not to take trainers out of the loop. It recommends and explains against a bar we approve; when it suggests changing that bar, one of us reviews and signs off before anything changes.
